Definitions

The principle of conservation of charge : The net charge of an isolated system remains constant during any physical process
(Q=(Q1+Q2)/2)
The principle of charge quantization : Every charge in the universe consists of integer multiples of the electron charge
(Q=nq)

The magnitude of the electrostatic force extend by one (point) charge (Q1) on another point charge (Q2) is directly proportional to the product of the magnitudes of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of the idstance (r) between the their centres (them) A region of space in which an electric charge experiences a force. Q = (Q1+Q2)/2
  • The formula used when two objects come into contact, touch and seperate again.
  • This formula is used to find the final charge of either object after they seperate.
Q = unit of charge : Coulombs (C)
Q1 = initial charge of object 1
Q2 = initial charge of object 2

n = Q/qe
  • This formula is used when looking for the number of electrons.
  • We can use this to calculate the number of electrons added or removed to give an object charge.
  • Or we can use it to calculate how many electrons were transfered during contact.
n = Number of electrons
Q = Change in charge (Final - Initial)
qe = the charge on an electron (1.60217663 x 10^-19 coulombs)

Charge
  • Neutrally charged : equal protons and electrons and therefore the net charge is 0.
  • Positively charged : more protons than electrons and has a net positive charge (a deficit of electrons).
  • Negatively charged : more electrons than protons (excess of electrons) and has a net negative charge.
An object aquires a charge throught the transfer of electrons (NB - only electrons can be transfered)
  • An object aquires a negative charge by accepting electrons
  • An object aquires a positive charge by Donating electrons
electrons can be rubbed from one object onto another.
