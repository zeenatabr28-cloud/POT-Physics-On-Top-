# POT(Physics On Top)

DESCRIPTION
My personal physics studying website
Next year is my last year of high school and I plan to do the absolute best that I can, not just because I need to for my university application, but also because I want to achieve as much as I can in my last year.

The style I am going for is super fun and pastel, which is not usually what you would expect from a website dedicated to physics and chemistry, which is seen as more technical and intelectual, as if pink isn't just as scientific of a colour as lime green.

This is the colour Palette that I am using, massive thank you to LostInIndigo on Lospec
![The colour palette that I am using](iridescent-crystal-8x.png)

I love calm and chill colours and I feel like every difficult concept is easier to understand when paired with lavender.

The site will focus on the Physical Sciences Elective which consists of Physics and Chemistry.

The website will be divided into three main sections, which are the three years that go into the elective, and each of these sections consists of the topics of each grade, a library of exams and memos, and a flashcard tab.
I want to have video lessons that are playable within the site, sourced from Khan Academy and a few Youtubers who's videos I feel are helpful and informative.
